01 · Product Concept

Discovery-in-a-crisis, before anything else

In an emergency — an injured stray, a sick pet after hours — there's no reliable way to find a nearby, verified vet, ambulance or rescuer. Information is scattered across Google, Instagram and WhatsApp groups. That's the highest-intent, most frequent pain point, so it comes before routine browsing or booking.

Pet parents

Primary audience. Urban India, need fast access to trustworthy care and a running health record for their pet.

Rescuers & feeders

Secondary audience. Need the nearest ambulance or NGO for a street animal, fast, with no time for research.

Vets, NGOs & shelters

Supply side. Get discovered by the people already searching for them, without paying for ads.

03 · AI & Automation

Where AI actually carries weight

Two of these run as working demos in the prototype today (document extraction and symptom triage); the other two are the automation this data unlocks on the supply and NGO side.

Medical-document extraction

Processes: a photo of a prescription, vaccination card or lab report. Automates: manually re-typing medical history — the model extracts diagnosis, medicines and next-due dates straight into the pet's timeline, with reminders.

Symptom triage & routing

Processes: free-text symptoms plus species/age. Automates: deciding urgent vs. routine and routing to the right provider type, instead of a blind directory search.

Directory enrichment

Processes: unstructured listings pulled from Google, Instagram and WhatsApp Business. Automates: structuring, de-duplicating and flagging inconsistent entries — the manual ops work of building the directory.

Rescue-story copy assistant

Processes: a rescuer's raw notes and photos. Automates: drafting a publishable rescue story or fundraiser post.

Own ideas: the emergency-first framing, ranking SOS results by ETA + verification (never ads), and using document-extraction to build a longitudinal pet health record are original to this concept. The base "searchable directory" pattern is inspired by Practo/JustDial-style platforms, as named in the brief.
04 · Technology

How the pieces connect

Next.js on Vercel, Supabase for the database and auth, Claude for the two AI workflows above.

The API layer also calls out to Google Maps/Places, the WhatsApp Business API, Razorpay and Firebase Cloud Messaging as needed — not modelled as separate always-on services.

05 · MVP Thinking

30 days, Gurgaon-only

Enough to test the emergency-first bet with real providers, without building things a first cohort won't miss yet.

Day 1Day 21 — build freezeDay 30 — live

Build

Directory + geo-search, provider profiles, SOS flow, manual pet profile, and the AI document-upload feature — launched Gurgaon-only with 30–50 manually onboarded, verified listings.

Leave out

Booking & payments, native mobile apps, self-serve provider onboarding, reviews/ratings, AI chat triage (needs tuning), multi-city expansion.
