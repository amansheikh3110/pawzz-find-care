02

Visual asset plan

Right now every icon on the site is a plain line-drawn SVG — clean, but it reads as a dashboard, not something built to help an animal. This plan adds real illustration where it earns an emotional reaction, without touching the small functional icons that already work fine.

Icons vs. images — a recommendation, not just a list. Small UI icons (search, phone, star, tab-bar glyphs) should stay as hand-drawn SVG: they need to work crisply at 16–24px in any colour, and AI image generators are inconsistent at that scale. Where AI-generated art earns its place is the six emotional illustrations below — hero moments where a dog, not a line icon, is the point. I've included one exception: a brand mascot mark, since that's a single distinctive asset worth generating rather than hand-drawing.
On "animated." Most AI image tools (Midjourney, DALL·E, etc.) generate still frames. Send me stills as transparent PNGs and I'll add real motion in CSS — a gentle float, a gaze that shifts on scroll, a wagging-tail loop for the icon-sized paw mark. If you can also generate a short looping clip (via Runway/Pika/Kaiber) for the hero or donate image specifically, even better — I can drop that in as a background video loop instead of a still.
